Our mission

We believe a soldering iron and a good teacher can change a life.

Gears Initiative is a volunteer-run non-profit making practical engineering education free and accessible for students who've been left out — by geography, by income, by circumstance. We turn working engineers into teachers, and curiosity into capability.

photo · team & students

How it works

01
Engineers volunteer to teach

Professionals record short, project-based lessons in their field — from circuits to CAD.

02
We deliver it free, anywhere

Lessons stream on any device, work on low bandwidth, and never sit behind a paywall or sign-up.

03
Students build & get mentored

Donations fund hardware kits and the mentors who review what students create.
